Mechanical Rigor

Our 150-Point Inspection Standard

Before any car joins our showroom floor, it must pass these strict benchmarks:

1. Powertrain & Engine

  • ✓ Cylinder compression & diagnostic error scan
  • ✓ Transmission shifting smoothness & fluid level
  • ✓ Radiator cooling & water pump pressure
  • ✓ Battery health & alternator charging rate
  • ✓ Exhaust emissions & catalytic converter integrity

2. Safety, Brakes & Tires

  • ✓ Brake pads with minimum 50% life remaining
  • ✓ Brake rotors, lines, and ABS sensor verification
  • ✓ Tires with minimum 4/32" tread depth (or brand new)
  • ✓ Wheel alignment & balance test drive
  • ✓ Steering rack, tie rods, and suspension ball joints

3. Electronics, Cabin & Body

  • ✓ Air conditioning & dual climate heating performance
  • ✓ Infotainment, backup camera & radar safety sensors
  • ✓ Power windows, door locks, and keyless fobs
  • ✓ Complete interior sanitization & odor neutralization
  • ✓ Paint depth scan to confirm no frame structural damage
